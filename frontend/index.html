PolyTg is a machine-learning platform designed to estimate the glass transition temperature of polymers from their polymer SMILES representations. It combines polymer descriptors, ensemble learning, uncertainty estimation and applicability-domain assessment.

PLATFORM CAPABILITIES

From structure to property insight.

⌬

Polymer Representation

Accepts polymer SMILES structures and converts them into a structured set of polymer-level descriptors.

◈

Tg Prediction

Uses a trained ExtraTrees regression model to estimate polymer glass transition temperature in Kelvin and Celsius.

◎

Prediction Assessment

Presents a conformal prediction interval and an applicability-domain assessment alongside predictions.

PolyTg uses a conformal prediction configuration to construct a prediction interval. A nearest-neighbour distance assessment provides an additional indication of how closely the input resembles the model's training domain.

Important limitations

These results describe the current evaluation and should not be interpreted as a guarantee of performance on every polymer. Performance under polymer-class grouping was lower than the random test split. The applicability-domain indicator is a caution flag, not a probability of correctness. Predictions should be treated as research estimates rather than experimental measurements.
